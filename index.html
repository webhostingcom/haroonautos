AUTOMOTIVE ENGINEERING GUIDANCE

Better vehicle care. Better performance.

Important vehicle maintenance and inspection considerations every vehicle owner should understand to support safety, reliability and long-term performance.

Regular servicing and preventive maintenance help identify worn components, maintain vehicle performance and reduce the likelihood of unexpected breakdowns.

Proper vehicle inspections and diagnostic checks help identify mechanical and electrical faults before they develop into more serious problems.

Pay attention to unusual engine sounds, warning indicators, overheating and changes in performance. Professional inspection can help identify the cause.

Braking and suspension components should be inspected regularly to help maintain vehicle control, stability and safe driving performance.

Replacement components should be appropriate for the vehicle's make, model and specifications to support reliable operation.

After repairs and servicing, appropriate checks should be carried out to confirm that the work has addressed the reported issue before the vehicle is returned.
